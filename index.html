If you can only manage five bites, make every one count.

Little Bowl is a nutrition companion for people with low appetite, for example during GLP-1 therapy. Protein in two taps, water in glasses, fiber and how you feel. No calorie counting.

Developed with a Swiss dietitian.

Coming soon to the App Store For iPhone, in English and German.

Small amounts, counted simply.

When eating is hard, it helps to see what you managed. Everything here is free.

Protein in two taps
Pick from a list of 43 everyday portions, from Greek yogurt to lentils. No calorie counting.
Water in glasses
One tap for each glass of 250 ml.
Fiber
Counted along with the portions you log.
Strength training
Note your strength sessions through the week.
How you feel
Note how you feel and any symptoms you have.
Your therapy plan
The medication, dose and rhythm your doctor prescribed, with a reminder, injection sites and your pen supply. The app records your plan; it never suggests a dose.
Weight and waist
For your own overview and your report. Apple Health is optional and starts only after you tap Connect Apple Health: steps and weights from your scale come in, water, protein, fiber and weight go out.
Tips for difficult days
General tips for days when eating is hard. The same for everyone, and no replacement for your dietitian.

One report for your next appointment.

Little Bowl turns what you logged into a PDF for your dietitian or doctor: doses, weight, protein, water, fiber, strength training, steps and how you felt.

Everything in it is what you entered yourself, shown without judging. It isn’t a diagnosis.

In Switzerland: ready for your control appointments.

The Swiss insurance companion shows when the control appointments for your health insurance are due, so you can bring the report to each one.

  1. StartThe day your therapy begins, entered in Settings.
  2. Week 16The first control appointment.
  3. Month 10The second control appointment.
  4. Every 6 monthsThe controls after that.

Read the privacy policy

A companion, not medical advice.

Little Bowl records, reminds and exports. It gives no medical advice, never suggests or changes a dose, and doesn’t judge whether your therapy works. It doesn’t replace your doctor or your dietitian; questions about your therapy belong with them.

In an emergency, call your local emergency number (911 in the US, 112 in the EU, 144 in Switzerland).
