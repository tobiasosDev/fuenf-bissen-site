Help and contact

Write to info@tecminds.ch. We answer in English or German.

Please don’t send us your entries or health details by email. We don’t need them to help, and the app keeps them on your phone for a reason.

Little Bowl gives no medical advice, never suggests or changes a dose, and doesn’t judge whether your therapy works. It doesn’t replace your doctor or your dietitian. In an emergency, call your local emergency number (911 in the US, 112 in the EU, 144 in Switzerland).

Common questions

Is my data safe? Where is it stored?

Only on your iPhone. There is no account and no server, and the app doesn’t sync through iCloud, use analytics, show ads or include tracking. We never receive what you log, so we can’t see it. Backups, the CSV export and the PDF report are files you create and share yourself. Details are in the privacy policy.

How do I move to a new iPhone?

On the old iPhone, open Settings in the app and tap Download a backup, then save the file to Files or iCloud Drive, or send it to yourself by AirDrop. On the new iPhone, open Settings in the app and tap Restore a backup. Your iPhone’s own backup through iCloud or a computer also includes Little Bowl.

How do I cancel the free week?

On your iPhone, open Settings › your name › Subscriptions and choose Little Bowl. If you cancel at least 24 hours before the free week ends, you aren’t charged. The app reminds you two days before it ends. Purchases and cancellations go through Apple.

I bought Pro. How do I get it back?

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchases while signed in with the same Apple Account.

Which therapies does it support?

Injections and tablets of the GLP-1 class, as your doctor prescribed them. The app only records what your doctor prescribed: the medication, the dose and the rhythm. It doesn’t recommend a medication.

Does the app give dose advice?

No. It never suggests, calculates or changes a dose, and it doesn’t judge whether your therapy works. The reminder follows the plan you entered. Questions about your dose belong with your doctor.

How is the protein target calculated?

1.2 g of protein per kg of adjusted body weight a day, and at least 60 g, after the EASO/EFAD/ECPO consensus 2026. If your dietitian gave you a target, theirs comes first: enter it in the app and Little Bowl uses it instead.

Which languages does the app speak?

English and German. The app follows your iPhone’s language, and you can switch it in the app’s Settings.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app. We hold no copy, so there is nothing to delete on our side. Backup files, CSV exports and reports you saved yourself stay where you put them.
