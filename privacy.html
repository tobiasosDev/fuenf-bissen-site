Privacy policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Little Bowl (in German: Fünf Bissen) does not collect personal data. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. What you log stays on your iPhone.

Who is responsible

TecMinds GmbH, Neustadtstrasse 8a, 6003 Luzern, Switzerland. Email: info@tecminds.ch.

What stays on your device

Everything you enter is stored only inside the app on your iPhone:

  • what you eat and drink: the portions you log with their protein and fiber, and your glasses of water;
  • your strength training sessions;
  • how you feel: symptoms and notes;
  • your therapy plan as your doctor prescribed it: medication, dose, rhythm, reminder time, injection sites and pen supply, and the doses and appointments you log;
  • your weight and waist;
  • your settings, such as your targets, your therapy start and the app language.

We have no server and never receive this data. The app doesn’t sync it through iCloud. It is part of your device backup if you back up your iPhone with iCloud or a computer; Apple handles that backup under your settings.

Health information

Your entries are health information. Because they never reach us, we don’t process, analyse or sell them, and we can’t see or recover them.

Apple Health

Connecting Apple Health is optional. Only when you tap Connect Apple Health does the app ask iOS for permission, and then it reads one thing: your daily step count. It never writes anything to Apple Health. Your steps are not sent to us or anyone else and are never used for advertising. They appear in a file only if you create the PDF report yourself, which can include your steps. You can turn off access at any time in the Health app under Sharing › Apps.

When you share something

The app creates a file only when you ask for it: a backup (JSON), the CSV export or the PDF report. It then opens the iOS share sheet. Where the file goes, for example to Files, by AirDrop, or by mail to your dietitian or doctor, is your choice, and the privacy policy of that service or person applies. A backup contains everything you logged, so keep it somewhere safe.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple. We don’t receive your name, Apple Account or payment details. Apple gives us anonymised sales reports.

App Store statistics

Apple gives developers aggregated statistics, such as downloads and crash reports, only from people who chose to share analytics with developers in their device settings. These statistics don’t identify you and contain nothing you logged in the app.

Notifications

If you turn on the dose reminder, or start the free week of Pro, the app schedules local notifications on your iPhone: a reminder on your dose days and a reminder two days before the free week ends. Nothing is sent to us.

No tracking, no advertising

The app contains no advertising identifiers, no analytics or advertising SDKs and no tracking pixels. We don’t share anything with advertisers or data brokers.

This website

This website uses no cookies and no analytics, and it loads nothing from third parties: no fonts, scripts or images from elsewhere. It is hosted by GitHub Pages; GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses for security. See the GitHub privacy statement.

Your rights

Under the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (revDSG) and, where it applies, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you can ask us what personal data we hold about you and have it corrected or deleted. Because the app collects none, we normally hold none. If you write to us by email, we use your message only to answer it. Write to info@tecminds.ch.

To delete everything the app stores, delete the app from your iPhone. Backup files, CSV exports and reports you made yourself stay where you saved them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and the date above. If the app ever starts collecting data, we will say so here before that version is released.
