A companion, not medical advice

Little Bowl helps you keep track of what you eat and drink when your appetite is low, and of the therapy your doctor prescribed. It is not a medical device. It doesn’t diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition, and it doesn’t replace advice from your doctor or dietitian.

  • The app gives no medical advice. It never suggests, calculates or changes a dose, and it doesn’t judge whether your therapy works.
  • The therapy plan in the app is what you enter from your own prescription. The reminder follows that plan. Questions about your medication or dose belong with your doctor.
  • The targets for protein, water and fiber are general nutrition targets. If your dietitian gave you a protein target, theirs comes first; you can enter it in the app.
  • The tips are general and the same for everyone. The PDF report shows what you logged; it isn’t a diagnosis or a medical assessment.
  • If you feel unwell, talk to your doctor. In an emergency, call your local emergency number (911 in the US, 112 in the EU, 144 in Switzerland).

Free and Pro

Free: all tracking, the reminders, the tips, backups and the CSV export. Pro adds the PDF report and the Swiss insurance companion.

Yearly subscription

  • $29.99 a year in the US. Apple sets the price in other countries; the app shows your price before you buy.
  • New subscribers get a free week. Payment is charged to your Apple Account when the free week ends, unless you cancel at least 24 hours before. The app reminds you two days before the free week ends.
  • The subscription renews automatically each year unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the period ends.
  • Manage or cancel any time in the iPhone’s Settings › your name › Subscriptions.

Restore earlier purchases on a new device with Restore purchases in the app’s Settings. Purchases are processed by Apple, and refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your data

Everything you log is stored only on your iPhone. We never receive it, so we can’t restore it for you. Download a backup in the app’s Settings and keep it in Files or iCloud Drive before you switch or reset your phone.
